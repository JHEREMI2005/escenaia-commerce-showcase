# EscenaIA Commerce

**Shopify e-commerce and digital product ecosystem connected to EscenaIA Studio**  
**Status:** Pre-launch commercial development

EscenaIA Commerce is the commercial layer of the EscenaIA ecosystem. It is designed around Shopify for storefront, catalog, digital products, discovery, marketing, checkout, and access to the professional EscenaIA Studio experience.

This repository is a **public portfolio showcase**. It documents the product strategy, e-commerce architecture, marketing approach, and my role without exposing private theme code, credentials, payment configuration, customer data, or proprietary business logic.

## Ecosystem model

```text
Traffic / Marketing
      ↓
EscenaIA Commerce (Shopify)
      ↓
Storefront · Catalog · Digital Products · Marketplace
      ↓
Checkout / Commercial Journey
      ↓
EscenaIA Studio
      ↓
Professional AI-assisted production workflows
```

The product strategy keeps Shopify focused on the commercial experience while the professional production workflow remains in EscenaIA Studio.

## Relationship with EscenaIA Studio

EscenaIA Commerce and EscenaIA Studio are intentionally separated:

**Commerce** focuses on acquisition, discovery, products, commercial pages and checkout.

**Studio** focuses on the professional AI-assisted audiovisual production workflow.

This separation lets the commercial surface evolve without forcing the professional application to behave like a Shopify theme.
